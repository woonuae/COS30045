Understanding Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances are one of the biggest contributors to residential electricity use in Australia. This site explores publicly available energy rating data to help everyday Australians make more informed choices when buying new appliances — starting with televisions.

Use the navigation bar above to explore our findings on television energy consumption, or learn more about this project on the About Us page.
